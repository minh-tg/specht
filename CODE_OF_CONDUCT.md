# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and behavior without attacking people.
- Accept good-faith corrections and differing viewpoints.
- Keep project spaces focused on the work.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or
imagery, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Public or
private conduct that materially harms project participants may be considered.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub.
Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as the
circumstances allow. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation,
or take other action they consider appropriate.

This code of conduct applies wherever someone is representing the project or
participating in its community spaces.
